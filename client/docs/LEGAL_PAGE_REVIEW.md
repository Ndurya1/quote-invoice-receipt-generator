# Legal page review checklist

The client exposes public Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages at `/privacy` and `/terms`. The pages describe the current DocuFlow MVP behavior: account and business data, client and document content, authenticated PDF downloads, local onboarding drafts, owner-scoped records, and the fact that DocuFlow does not process payments or natively send email or WhatsApp messages.

Before production launch, the business owner or legal adviser should confirm:

- the legal entity and controller identity behind the DocuFlow service;
- the privacy contact address and any physical contact details required by the applicable jurisdiction;
- the lawful bases for each processing purpose;
- actual hosting, infrastructure, authentication, storage, monitoring, and support providers;
- international transfer safeguards and subprocessor disclosures;
- retention periods for accounts, documents, logs, backups, and deleted records;
- the process and response times for access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, and portability requests;
- the security incident and breach-notification process;
- governing law, dispute forum, and any consumer or business-specific terms; and
- whether registration with or notification to the relevant data-protection authority is required.

The page copy is intentionally limited to behavior currently represented in the application. New analytics, uploads, messaging providers, payments, or account features require a corresponding privacy-policy review before release.
